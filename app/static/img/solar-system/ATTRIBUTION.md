# Planetary texture credits

Source: [Solar System Scope — Solar Textures](https://www.solarsystemscope.com/textures/), by INOVE / Solar System Scope.
License: [Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 International](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/).
Downloaded 2026-11-14. Maps use NASA imagery and elevation data; they are representative visual maps, not scientific measurements.

Adaptations: the planetary maps were resized to 1024 × 512 and encoded as WebP. The sky map keeps its 2048 × 1024 resolution as WebP. Saturn's RGBA ring map is preserved as PNG. Rendered with spherical projection, rotation, simulated sunlight, atmosphere and shadows.
